Assad Khan has spent more than fifteen years at the intersection of technology delivery, business operations, and enterprise client relationships — across contact centre operations, managed IT, cybersecurity, AI transformation, and building intelligence. He has built and led teams across Canada, the United States, the Philippines, and the Middle East, working directly with C-suite executives, institutional clients, and government organizations on engagements that range from custom ERP deployments to 24/7 managed security operations.

As Director of Operations for North America at Six Eleven BPO — one of the Philippines' largest contact centre and outsourcing operations with over 6,000 employees across seven facilities — Assad leads North American business development and client strategy, working directly with the CEO to originate and structure enterprise client engagements across Canada and the United States. That role, held since 2016, gave Assad an operational view of what enterprise clients actually need from a technology and services partner: not a pitch, but accountability, consistency, and delivery that improves over time.

Prior to his operational leadership role, Assad spent six years as Vice President of Business Development at Empire Global Solutions Inc., a North American BPO and managed services firm. In that role he managed enterprise client relationships across financial services, healthcare, and telecommunications — building long-term partnerships where customer experience, technology reliability, and commercial outcomes were all part of the same conversation. The lesson he carried forward: the organizations that win long-term are the ones who treat technology as a business outcome, not a procurement event.

SBTech was founded in 2021 out of a clear gap Assad kept encountering in the Canadian market. Organizations were being sold technology — cybersecurity tools, ERP platforms, AI solutions — by firms that implemented and disappeared. Clients were left with systems they didn't fully understand, vendors they couldn't hold accountable, and no one to call at 3am when something went wrong. SBTech was built specifically to close that gap: senior-led delivery, managed operations that continue after implementation, and a team that treats every client's environment as if it were their own.

Today SBTech delivers managed IT and cybersecurity, custom ERP and systems integration, AI governance and training programs, and white-label technology delivery across Canada, the United States, the Middle East, and offshore. Assad also leads Smart Building Technology Inc., focused on EV infrastructure, smart building systems, and intelligent facility operations — bringing the same delivery philosophy to the physical built environment.

"We don't scope a project and disappear. Every engagement moves from implementation into managed operations — because technology that isn't actively managed is technology that eventually fails."
— Assad Khan, Founder & CEO, SBTech Inc.

Our Story

Why SBTech exists

The Canadian technology market is full of companies that will sell you a security platform, implement an ERP, or build an AI program — and then hand you a manual and a support ticket number. SBTech was founded specifically to change that experience.

Assad Khan started SBTech in 2021 after more than a decade of watching enterprise clients across financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, and the public sector struggle with the same pattern: significant technology investments, minimal accountability after go-live, and no senior team to call when things didn't go as planned. The tools were often right. The delivery and the ongoing support were almost always wrong.

SBTech was built around a different operating model. Every engagement is senior-led from day one. Implementation moves directly into managed operations — the same team that builds your environment monitors it, maintains it, and improves it. And every deliverable is audit-ready: NIST-aligned, ISO-structured, and documented in a way that holds up under regulatory scrutiny.

That model — senior delivery, managed continuity, full accountability — is what SBTech brings to every client, regardless of size, vertical, or geography.
